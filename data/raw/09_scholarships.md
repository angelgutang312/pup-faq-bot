# Scholarships and Financial Assistance

PUP offers several forms of financial assistance to students, including academic scholarships tied to grade thresholds (commonly requiring a general weighted average around 1.75–2.0 or better, depending on the specific scholarship program), entrance scholarships for outstanding incoming freshmen, and other assistance programs such as student loan funds, book banks, and cultural or student assistantship programs that let students work in exchange for support.

Students interested in applying should coordinate with their college's scholarship coordinator or the university's Scholarship and Financial Assistance Office, since application periods, required documents, and specific GWA cutoffs vary by scholarship type and are announced each semester.
