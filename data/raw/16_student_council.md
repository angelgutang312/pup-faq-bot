# PUP Student Council

The PUP Student Council (PUP SC) is the official representative body of the student population, mandated to organize and lead campaigns and activities that promote and defend students' rights and general welfare, and to provide direct services to the student body. The Council is composed of the Central Student Council (also known as the Sentral na Konseho ng Mag-aaral or SKM), local student councils in each college (including the Open University System and Institute of Technology), and independent bodies covering audit, elections, accreditation of student organizations, and a student tribunal.

Elections for student council positions follow a first-past-the-post voting system, with officers serving a term of one academic year. Students can get involved by voting in scheduled elections, joining accredited student organizations, or running for a council seat when nominations open.
