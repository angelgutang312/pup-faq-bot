# Library Services and Clearance

PUP libraries (main and campus-level) provide students access to physical collections, reserved course materials, and in many cases online/digital resources and research databases. Students are generally required to present a valid ID and settle any outstanding library obligations, such as unreturned books or unpaid fines, before requesting graduation or transfer clearance.

Library hours, borrowing limits, and available digital resources vary by campus and are announced through the library's own official channels at the start of each academic year.
