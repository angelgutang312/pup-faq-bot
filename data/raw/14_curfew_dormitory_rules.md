# Curfew and Overnight Stay Rules

For students residing in or making use of on-campus facilities, a curfew of 10:00 PM is generally in effect. Any overnight stay on campus beyond this curfew requires prior approval from the appropriate university office (such as the college dean, student affairs office, or dormitory administration, depending on the campus). Students planning group activities, org meetings, or academic work that may run late should secure the necessary permissions in advance to avoid being in violation of this policy.
