# Tuition and Miscellaneous Fees

As a state university, PUP offers heavily subsidized tuition compared to private institutions, with many programs falling under free or reduced tuition schemes for qualified Filipino students under applicable national legislation (e.g., free tuition laws for state universities and colleges). Students are still generally responsible for certain miscellaneous and laboratory fees, which vary depending on the program and specific course requirements (e.g., laboratory-heavy programs in engineering, science, or computing may carry additional fees).

Payment deadlines are tied to the enrollment period each semester, and students with unresolved balances may face holds on services such as release of grades or clearance, even though (per examination policy) they cannot be barred from taking their exams over unpaid fees alone.
