# Dean's List and Academic Standing

Students are expected to maintain good academic standing, generally reflected by a General Weighted Average (GWA) of 2.0 or better for undergraduates. Falling consistently below the required standing may result in an academic warning, probation, or in repeated cases, dismissal from the program, depending on specific college and program rules.

Recognition such as the Dean's List is awarded to students who exceed specific GWA thresholds each semester (often in the 1.00–1.75 range depending on the college), along with meeting a minimum unit load and having no failing or incomplete grades in the term being evaluated.
