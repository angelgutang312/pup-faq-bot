# Shifting Programs and Transferring Campuses

Students who wish to shift from one degree program to another, or transfer from one PUP campus to another, must coordinate with the receiving college's Dean's Office and the Office of Admission Services. Requirements commonly include a minimum GWA, availability of slots in the target program, and completion of any bridging or deficiency subjects required to align the student's academic record with the new program's curriculum.

Shifting and transfer requests are typically processed within specific windows each academic year (often before the start of a new semester), so students are advised to inquire with their target college well ahead of the enrollment period.
