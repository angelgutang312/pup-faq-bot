# Academic Load, Prerequisite and Advanced Subjects

Each degree program prescribes a normal academic load (a set number of units) per semester. As a general rule, a student may not take an advanced subject until the prerequisite subject has been passed. In some cases, a student may be allowed to enroll in a prerequisite and its advanced/co-requisite subject in the same term, but only with the recommendation and approval of the department chairperson or college dean.

Graduating students in their last term may be granted an excess or overload — additional units beyond the normal load — at the discretion of the Vice President for Academic Affairs, typically capped at six (6) additional academic units, so they can complete remaining requirements and graduate on time.

Students who wish to change subjects, substitute a subject, or drop a subject after enrollment must file the appropriate request with their department, which then gets submitted to the Registrar's Office for processing.
