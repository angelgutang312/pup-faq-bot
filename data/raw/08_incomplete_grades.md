# Removing an Incomplete (INC) Mark

A student who receives an Incomplete (INC) mark must formally request its removal by submitting a Completion Form, along with supporting documents such as the faculty member's class record and the student's registration certificate, to the appropriate university office (typically routed through the college and the Office of the University Registrar).

The outstanding requirement (e.g., a missed major exam) must be completed within one year of the end of the term in which the INC was given. If the deadline lapses without completion, the INC is automatically converted to a failing grade under standard policy, so students are strongly advised not to delay.
