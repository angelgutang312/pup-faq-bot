# Student ID and Campus Entry Policy

Students are required to carry and present a valid PUP student identification card when entering campus, in line with the university's "No ID, No Entry" policy. However, a student who does not have their ID on hand should not automatically be denied entry, provided they can sufficiently show other proof that they are a bona fide student (e.g., a certificate of registration or another valid document).

Similar accommodation applies to uniform policy violations at campuses or colleges that require uniforms — proof of enrollment should still allow entry even without full compliance, though the student may still be referred for appropriate guidance regarding the policy.
