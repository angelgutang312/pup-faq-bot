# Enrollment Procedure for New and Continuing Students

Only students who have completed official enrollment are permitted to attend classes or be marked present in a class roster. New freshmen typically enroll after passing PUPCET or an equivalent admission evaluation, following a sequence of document submission, subject assignment/adviser approval, payment of applicable fees, and confirmation through the university's enrollment system.

Continuing students re-enroll each semester through the same online system, subject to being in good academic standing and having no unresolved holds (e.g., unpaid fees, missing clearance, incomplete grades that have lapsed). Enrollment periods are announced through official PUP channels ahead of each semester, and students are encouraged to enroll early since sections and slots for popular subjects fill up quickly.

Late enrollment is generally discouraged and may require special approval from the college dean's office, especially once regular classes have already started.
