# Student Rights and Freedom from Militarization

PUP's student handbook affirms that the pursuit of academic excellence and the exercise of academic freedom can only be achieved in an atmosphere free from fear and unreasonable restraint. In line with this, no military detachment or personnel may be installed inside the campus, except for the university's Reserve Officers' Training Corps (ROTC) program, which operates under its own separate regulatory framework.

This provision is part of a broader set of student rights recognized in the handbook, meant to protect the university as a space for open academic inquiry.
