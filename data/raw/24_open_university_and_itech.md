# Open University System (OUS) and Institute of Technology (iTech)

Beyond its traditional resident colleges, PUP also operates an Open University System (OUS) that offers flexible, distance, and blended learning programs for students who are unable to attend regular on-campus classes — for example, working students or those in different regions. The Institute of Technology (iTech) similarly offers a distinct track of technology-focused programs.

Both OUS and iTech maintain their own local student councils under the overall PUP Student Council structure, and while their delivery mode differs from the traditional resident program, they generally follow the same core university policies on grading, attendance-equivalent requirements, and academic standing, adapted to their respective learning formats.
