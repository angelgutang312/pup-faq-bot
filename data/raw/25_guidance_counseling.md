# Guidance, Counseling and Testing Services (GCTSO)

The Guidance, Counseling and Testing Services Office (GCTSO) supports students through counseling services, testing (such as psychological or aptitude assessments used in some admission and section-grouping processes), and issuance of official excuse slips for absences related to documented personal or medical circumstances.

Students experiencing academic stress, personal difficulties, or who simply want guidance on academic and career planning are encouraged to reach out to GCTSO, which is generally available through their college or campus's student affairs unit.
