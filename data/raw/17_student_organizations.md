# Joining and Forming Student Organizations

Recognized student organizations at PUP must go through an accreditation process facilitated by the Student Council Commission on Student Organizations and Accreditation (SC-COSOA), in coordination with the Office of Student Affairs. Accreditation typically requires submission of a constitution and by-laws, a list of officers and members, and an adviser from the faculty, among other requirements set for each academic year.

Students who want to join an existing organization can approach the org directly during recruitment periods (often at the start of a semester), while those who want to start a new organization should coordinate early with OSA and SC-COSOA, since the accreditation process can take time.
