# Student Conduct and Code of Behavior

As representatives of the university, PUP students are expected to uphold high standards of behavior both on and off campus. Students are expected to know and respect the university's philosophy, vision, mission, and core values, which emphasize developing well-rounded citizens and leaders through quality, accessible education.

Violations of the student code of conduct are handled through the appropriate disciplinary process, typically involving the Office of Student Affairs (OSA) and, where applicable, the Student Council's own disciplinary or tribunal bodies.
