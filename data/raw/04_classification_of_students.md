# Classification of Students

PUP classifies students based on their academic standing and load, such as regular students (those following the prescribed sequence and load per year level) and irregular students (those who are behind or ahead of the standard sequence, often due to failed, dropped, or overloaded subjects). Classification affects a student's priority during enrollment and which sections they are eligible to enroll in.

Detailed classification rules, along with admission policies for shifting between programs or colleges, are maintained by the respective College Dean's Office in coordination with the Office of Admission Services. Students who want to shift programs or transfer colleges should coordinate directly with these offices, as requirements vary by program and are subject to slot availability.
