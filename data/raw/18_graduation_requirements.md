# Graduation Requirements

To graduate, a student must complete all required units and subjects prescribed in their program's curriculum, meet the minimum academic standing required by their college, and settle all outstanding financial and clearance obligations (library, laboratory, department, etc.). Most programs also require an on-the-job training/practicum component and, depending on the program, a thesis, capstone project, or comprehensive exam.

Students nearing graduation should coordinate early with their department and the Registrar's Office to verify their curriculum checklist and confirm there are no missing requirements, since discrepancies discovered late can delay graduation and clearance for the commencement exercise.
