# Late Reporting and Correction of Grades

If a grade or mark was recorded incorrectly, the correction must generally be filed and processed within one semester from when the grade was originally received. Requests filed beyond this window may no longer be honored under standard policy, so students who spot a discrepancy on their grade record should report it to their instructor and the Registrar's Office as soon as possible.

Late reporting of grades (i.e., a faculty member submitting a grade past the official deadline) follows a separate university process and is typically routed through the department and Registrar's Office as well.
