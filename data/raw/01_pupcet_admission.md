# PUPCET and College Admission

The Polytechnic University of the Philippines College Entrance Test (PUPCET) is the standard admission requirement for incoming freshmen who want to enter a PUP campus, particularly the highly competitive Manila campus. Eligible takers include graduating senior high school students and recent graduates who have not yet enrolled in any college degree, diploma, or technical program. Applicants who have already taken college-level units are generally disqualified from taking the test as a freshman applicant.

Because PUP is a state university with heavily subsidized tuition, demand for slots far exceeds supply — historically only a small fraction of examinees nationwide are admitted to the Manila campus each year. When the standard entrance exam is suspended (as happened during the pandemic years), PUP has used alternative evaluation schemes such as the College Admission Evaluation (CAEPUP), processed through the university's online application portal, iApply.

PUP also grants entrance scholarships to outstanding senior high school graduates who have not enrolled elsewhere and can show strong academic performance, in lieu of or in addition to taking the entrance exam.
