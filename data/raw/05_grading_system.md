# Grading System

PUP uses a numeric grading scale ranging from 1.00 (Excellent, the highest) to 5.00 (Fail). For undergraduate programs, 3.00 is the lowest passing grade; grades between 3.25 and 5.00 are failing. Non-numeric marks are also used for special situations:

- **INC (Incomplete)** — given when a student is otherwise passing but has not completed a major requirement (e.g., a final exam or major project). The requirement must typically be completed within one year from the end of the term; otherwise the INC automatically converts to a failing grade.
- **W (Withdrawn)** — given when a student formally withdraws from a subject in writing, and must be filed no later than one month before the final examination period.
- **D (Dropped)** — given when a faculty member drops a student from the class roll for exceeding the allowed number of absences, or for never having attended the class.

A student who already earned a passing grade in a subject is not allowed to retake the examination solely to improve that grade. Graduate programs apply stricter passing standards than undergraduate programs (e.g., master's and doctoral students must maintain higher minimum weighted averages to remain in good standing).
