# Attendance Policy

Students are expected to attend all scheduled class sessions. Excused absences (e.g., due to illness or an official university-sanctioned activity) require supporting documentation, and in some cases an excuse slip issued through the Guidance, Counseling and Testing Services Office (GCTSO).

Importantly, an excused absence only excuses the student from being marked absent — it does not excuse them from the coursework. All lessons, activities, quizzes, or requirements covered during the missed session must still be completed by the student within a reasonable period set by the instructor.

Students who accumulate absences beyond the allowable limit for a subject (typically tied to a percentage of total class hours) may be given a Dropped (D) mark by the instructor.
