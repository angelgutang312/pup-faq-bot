# Dropping a Subject or Withdrawing from the University

A student who wants to drop a subject must file the appropriate request through their department, to be submitted to the Registrar's Office for processing, generally before a set cutoff date each semester (dropping after this date without approval may instead result in a failing grade). A Withdrawn (W) mark is used specifically when the student formally withdraws in writing, and this request must be filed no less than one month before the final examination period for that subject.

Withdrawing from the university entirely (a leave of absence or full withdrawal) follows a separate clearance process involving the college, the Registrar, and other offices depending on the student's obligations at the time.
