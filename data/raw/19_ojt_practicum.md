# On-the-Job Training (OJT) / Practicum

Most undergraduate programs at PUP require students to complete an On-the-Job Training or practicum period, giving them hands-on industry experience relevant to their field before graduation. Requirements typically include securing an accredited or approved host company/organization, completing a minimum number of training hours, and submitting required documentation (such as a training plan, evaluation from the host supervisor, and a narrative report) to the OJT coordinator of their department.

Students are advised to start coordinating with their OJT/practicum coordinator at least a semester in advance, since host company placement, endorsement letters, and required clearances can take time to process.
